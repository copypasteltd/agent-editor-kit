Changes: Python/NumPy tile execution, public Float32 straight RGBA, linear
premultiplied alpha, exact endpoint contract, rational author clock binding,
bounded memory admission, cancellation and validation. Native integer xfade
colour arithmetic and platform-specific wind hashes are not bit-identical.
Agent dissolve/crossfade aliases mean a continuous fade, not FFmpeg's native
stochastic dissolve. Dip black/white use an explicit authored cut fraction.
